⚠️ Photosensitivity warning

This app flashes the screen between light and dark at 0.5–40 times per second. Flashing at these rates (especially 3–30 Hz) can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, even with no prior history.

  • Do not use the flash if you or anyone who may see the screen has epilepsy or a history of seizures.
  • Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, nauseous, disoriented, or notice twitching or vision changes.
  • Use a lower intensity, a lit room, and sit back from the screen. Don’t use it while driving.
  • Press Esc or tap the screen at any time to stop everything.
